    Scope and honesty

    This hub is built from SailPoint's published exam study guides, the public product and developer documentation, and detailed practitioner notes taken after sitting both exams. It is a study aid, not a question dump, and it is not affiliated with or endorsed by SailPoint.

    Practice questions are written from the documented behaviour of the products against the published objectives. They are not reproductions of live exam items.

    The Field Review section reconstructs topics that came up, with answers worked out from documentation and, where available, from SailPoint's own written responses. Items still uncertain are tagged Open rather than presented as settled.
